ABOUT ME,
PONTES

Motion Design Sport Automotive Adobe Suite Figma Keyshot 9 Brazil 🇧🇷

4 years crafting sport & automotive identities.

For the past four years, I have worked as a designer and motion designer, entering the sports and automotive sectors. I have always been passionate about cars and soccer, having had the opportunity to work alongside Motorgrid Brasil (the largest supercar group in Latin America) as art director and soccer clubs such as Akademisk Boldklub (Danish club) as a motion designer. Today, I work with multiple programs, from the Adobe suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, InDesign, etc.) to complementary programs such as Figma (for prototyping) and Keyshot 9 (for 3D rendering).
